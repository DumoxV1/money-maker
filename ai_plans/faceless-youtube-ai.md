# Business Kit: Faceless YouTube with AI Narration

## Problem
Creators want video ad-income without showing their face, filming, or editing for hours — the traditional barrier to a YouTube channel.

## Audience
You, as the autonomous operator. Viewers are people in evergreen niches: history explainers, personal-finance breakdowns, how-things-work, language learning.

## Offer
A faceless channel where AI generates the script, an AI voice (ElevenLabs) narrates it, stock footage / AI visuals are assembled in CapCut, and the video is uploaded + optimized. Topics are chosen by an AI trend-scanner; the channel runs daily shorts + 2 longforms/week.

## Pricing
Ad revenue + sponsors; $100-$3000/month at scale (YouTube Partner threshold ~1k subs / 4k watch-hours first). Pricing psychology: sell the *system*, not the channel — bundle into a "faceless channel in a box" product later for $49.

## Channels
- YouTube (primary, longform + Shorts)
- TikTok repurposing (same clips)
- Pinterest idea pins linking to videos

## Free Tool Stack (0 budget)
- Hermes (script + topic research)
- ElevenLabs free tier (voice)
- CapCut (edit/assemble)
- Pexels/Pixabay (free stock)
- YouTube Data API (upload)

## 7-Day Launch Plan
- **Day 1** — Choose niche (e.g. "weird history facts"). Create channel + branding with free Canva.
- **Day 2** — AI writes 7 short scripts + 2 longform scripts.
- **Day 3** — Generate voices, assemble 7 Shorts in CapCut.
- **Day 4** — Upload 3 Shorts; set thumbnails via AI.
- **Day 5** — Write + assemble first longform; upload.
- **Day 6** — Post 4 more Shorts; engage with 20 comments in niche.
- **Day 7** — Review analytics; double down on the best-performing format.

## AI Execution
An AI agent selects trending topics, writes the script, synthesizes narration, assembles the video from stock + AI visuals, uploads via the YouTube Data API, and A/B tests titles/thumbnails. It monitors retention and retires low-performers. A human only approves the channel's bank details.

## One-Line Pitch
A faceless channel that prints ad revenue on autopilot.
