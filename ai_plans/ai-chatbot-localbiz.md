# Business Kit: AI Chatbot for Local Businesses

Local businesses (dentists, salons, plumbers, gyms) lose leads after hours and on weekends because no one answers.

## Audience
Small local service businesses in your city that live on inbound calls/bookings.

## Offer
An AI builds + hosts a no-code booking/FAQ chatbot with handover to the owner, and handles the whole onboarding via email automation.

## Pricing
€250 setup + €49/month care. Pricing psychology: the monthly fee is the anchor — setup feels like a one-time win.

## Channels

## 7-Day Launch Plan
- **Day 1** — Scrape 50 local businesses lacking chat/booking.
- **Day 2** — AI drafts 3 outreach email variants.
- **Day 3** — Send 20 cold emails.
- **Day 4** — Build the bot template (FAQ + booking).
- **Day 5** — Close 1 pilot; deploy.
- **Day 6** — Onboard via automated email sequence.
- **Day 7** — Case-study the pilot; use it in next 30 emails.

## AI Execution
An AI agent scrapes local businesses, drafts outreach, builds the bot, deploys it, and runs onboarding email automation. Semi-autonomous — a human closes the pilot call.
