# Business Kit: AI Engine-Ready Low-Poly Asset Packs

## Problem
Indie game and AR creators need coherent 3D prop sets, but modeling, retopology, LOD creation, collision setup, and multi-format export QA can consume days. Generic AI-generated models are usually isolated, inconsistent, and not ready to drop into an engine.

## Audience
Solo game developers, game-jam teams, no-code AR creators, and small studios that need affordable themed low-poly props rather than bespoke art.

## Offer
Original themed packs of 20-40 coordinated props, generated and processed by AI. Every pack has a consistent palette and scale, PBR textures, sensible pivots and names, simple collision meshes, LODs, validated GLB/FBX/OBJ files, preview renders, an import guide, and a provenance/license manifest.

## Pricing
€12 for a 10-prop starter pack, €29-39 for a 20-40 prop pack, or €79-149 for a multi-pack themed world. Offer one clearly licensed free sampler to demonstrate engine readiness without devaluing the full catalog.

## Channels
- itch.io asset marketplace
- Gumroad or Lemon Squeezy storefront
- CGTrader/Sketchfab only where current rules permit AI assets and the listing is labeled accurately
- Indie-game, game-jam, Blender, Unity, Godot, and Unreal communities
- Short automated turntable demos on YouTube, Pinterest, and social feeds

## Tool Stack
- Hermes for demand mining, briefs, orchestration, copy, support, and reporting
- Meshy or Tripo API for original mesh and texture generation
- Blender Python for normalization, retopology, LODs, collisions, exports, and renders
- Khronos glTF Validator plus scripted geometry/performance checks
- itch.io butler and a supported storefront API for versioned digital delivery

## 7-Day Launch Plan
- **Day 1** — Mine public indie-game discussions for recurring, non-franchise prop gaps. Select one narrow pack such as low-poly repair-shop clutter and write machine-checkable limits for style, scale, polygon count, texture size, and formats.
- **Day 2** — Generate original concept sheets and meshes from generic prompts. Reject outputs that resemble protected characters, logos, signature product designs, or training references supplied by third parties.
- **Day 3** — Run the Blender pipeline: orient and scale meshes, repair geometry, standardize names and pivots, create LODs and simple collisions, bake PBR textures, and export GLB/FBX/OBJ variants.
- **Day 4** — Validate every artifact for openability, missing textures, non-manifold geometry, triangle and texture budgets, naming, scale, and archive completeness. Regenerate failures automatically.
- **Day 5** — Render consistent turntables and contact sheets, generate the import guide and provenance/license manifest, and assemble a free sampler plus paid pack.
- **Day 6** — Publish accurate, AI-disclosed listings with screenshots, technical specifications, license terms, refund/support details, and versioned downloads on the permitted storefronts.
- **Day 7** — Schedule useful demo content, release the sampler in relevant communities without spam, collect structured compatibility reports, and queue the next complementary pack from actual demand.

## AI Execution
A scheduled AI agent gathers demand signals from public, terms-compliant sources and converts them into a constrained pack brief. It calls a 3D-generation API with original, non-infringing prompts, then invokes Blender headlessly to repair and normalize geometry, standardize scale and pivots, generate LODs and collisions, bake textures, export each supported format, and render previews. Scripted validators reject files that fail topology, performance, texture, archive, or openability checks and send only the failed asset back through the pipeline. For passing packs, the agent creates documentation and provenance records, publishes or updates storefront listings through supported APIs/CLIs, fulfills downloads automatically, answers scoped support questions, and turns compatibility feedback into patches. No manual modeling, physical presence, or operator fulfillment is required.

## Legal and Quality Guardrails
- Generate only generic, original subject matter; block brand names, logos, protected characters, celebrity likenesses, and prompts that request a living artist's signature style.
- Use generation providers and texture inputs whose current terms grant commercial output rights; retain prompt, model, date, source, and license records for each asset.
- Disclose AI involvement wherever a marketplace requires it, obey each marketplace's current upload rules, and do not list where generated assets are prohibited.
- Sell a clear commercial-use license for the files, not exclusivity or copyright guarantees that cannot be supported.
- Run malware, archive, format, missing-texture, geometry, scale, and performance checks before every release; never market an unvalidated generation as engine-ready.
- Respect robots directives, platform terms, rate limits, community rules, and outreach opt-outs when gathering demand or promoting packs.
