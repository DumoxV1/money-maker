# Business Kit: AI White-Label CMS Publishing Ops

## Problem
SEO agencies and content teams lose billable hours copying approved drafts into client CMSs, fixing headings and media, adding metadata and internal links, and checking the rendered page. The work is repetitive but too important to skip.

## Audience
Small SEO/content agencies, freelance strategists, and multi-site publishers with approved article backlogs and supported CMS access.

## Offer
A white-label AI publishing desk that converts approved drafts into QA-checked WordPress, Webflow, or Ghost posts. It handles formatting, taxonomy, metadata, schema, internal links, rights-safe images and alt text, rendered-preview checks, scheduling, and a delivery log.

## 7-Day Launch Plan
- **Day 1** — Pick one narrow beachhead: WordPress publishing for local-SEO agencies. Define the accepted draft and style-guide schema.
- **Day 2** — Build the draft-to-CMS pipeline in draft mode with least-privilege credentials and a full change log.
- **Day 3** — Add heading, link, metadata, image-license, mobile-preview, and rollback checks; run it on five owned demo posts.
- **Day 4** — Publish an anonymized before/after sample and a one-page scope stating that only client-approved copy is published.
- **Day 5** — Wire tiered Stripe checkout, CMS connection, intake, status emails, and credential revocation instructions.
- **Day 6** — Have the agent identify 30 relevant agencies from public business pages and send compliant, personalized sample offers with a clear opt-out.
- **Day 7** — Fulfill the first draft-mode sample automatically, deliver its QA log, and route the prospect to self-serve monthly onboarding.

## AI Execution
After a client connects a least-privilege CMS credential and submits an approved draft, the agent validates the input and style guide, formats the article, selects only rights-safe media, writes alt text, sets taxonomy, metadata, schema and internal links, checks the rendered preview with Playwright, schedules publication via the CMS API, and sends a QA log. It pauses rather than guessing when validation fails and retains a rollback record. Prospecting, opt-out handling, checkout, onboarding, fulfillment, reporting, and renewal reminders are automated, so no operator labor or physical presence is required.

## Legal and Quality Guardrails
- Publish only copy the client marks approved; never invent testimonials, claims, citations, or author credentials.
- Use client-owned or explicitly licensed media and store the source/license record.
- Use least-privilege CMS credentials, redact secrets from logs, and provide immediate revocation and deletion paths.
- Respect anti-spam rules, platform terms, rate limits, robots directives, and every outreach opt-out.
- Default new clients to CMS draft mode until their intake rules explicitly authorize scheduling or publication.
