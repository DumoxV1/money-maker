# Business Kit: Boring Micro-SaaS (one person)

## Problem
Solo firms need tiny, specific automation (invoicing reminders, cash-flow snapshots, tax deadlines) that no giant SaaS covers and they won't build themselves.

## Audience
Freelancers, small accounting practices, and trades businesses.

## Offer
A narrow $9-29/month tool that solves one painful, frequent, measurable workflow — built and marketed entirely by AI.

## Pricing
$2k+/month at scale (recurring). Pricing psychology: price for the pain relieved per month, not the lines of code.

## Channels
- Indie Hackers
- ProductHunt
- SEO blog
- Reddit niche subs

## Free Tool Stack (0 budget)
- No-code (Bubble/Softr)
- Stripe
- Airtable
- Zapier
- Hermes (build + copy)

## 7-Day Launch Plan
- **Day 1** — Pick one painful workflow (e.g. "freelance late-invoice reminder").
- **Day 2** — AI scopes the MVP; builds it in no-code.
- **Day 3** — Wire Stripe + Airtable.
- **Day 4** — Write the landing + copy (AI).
- **Day 5** — Launch on ProductHunt + Indie Hackers.
- **Day 6** — Post a useful thread in 2 Reddit subs.
- **Day 7** — Reply to every comment; ship a fix from feedback.

## AI Execution
An AI agent scopes the tool, builds the no-code app, writes the copy, sets up Stripe, and markets it via autoposts. It iterates from user feedback. An autonomous build + launch.

## One-Line Pitch
One boring problem, solved forever, $9/month, compounding.
