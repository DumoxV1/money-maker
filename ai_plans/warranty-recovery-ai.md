# Business Kit: AI Warranty & Rebate Recovery

## Problem
People overpay for repairs that were actually covered by warranty, or forget mail-in rebates they earned — leaving real money on the table.

## Audience
Busy homeowners, fleet owners, and parents with many appliances/electronics.

## Offer
An AI service that ingests a client's purchase history (from statements/emails), matches it against a warranty/rebate database, and files the claims — taking 15-25% of what it recovers.

## Pricing
15-25% of recovered value. Pricing psychology: contingency again — "we found €400 you already owned, keep 80%."

## Channels
- Nextdoor
- Facebook groups
- Cold email to past buyers
- CPA / tax-prep referral

## Free Tool Stack (0 budget)
- Spreadsheets (match logic)
- Retailer claim portals
- Calendly (intake)
- Hermes (draft claims)

## 7-Day Launch Plan
- **Day 1** — Build the warranty/rebate rule database (common retailers).
- **Day 2** — Write the intake (upload statements/emails).
- **Day 3** — Post in a local Facebook group: "you may be owed € from old receipts."
- **Day 4** — Landing page on GitHub Pages.
- **Day 5** — Cold-email 20 past buyers offering a free audit.
- **Day 6** — Run 3 pilot recoveries.
- **Day 7** — Ask for referrals; set up a simple referral bonus.

## AI Execution
An AI agent ingests statements, matches purchases to active warranty/rebate programs, drafts and submits the claims via portal automation, and logs recoveries. A human only handles escalations. Autonomous recovery.

## One-Line Pitch
We found €400 you already owned. Keep 80%, we take 20%.
