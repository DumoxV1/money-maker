# Business Kit: AI White-Label SEO Audit Reseller

## Problem
Agencies and freelancers need client-ready SEO audits but lack the time to produce branded, prioritized reports — so they lose pitches and retainers.

## Audience
Small SEO/marketing agencies, freelancers, and web designers who serve local businesses.

## Offer
An AI that crawls a prospect's site, generates a branded, prioritized fix-list PDF, and resells it under the agency's name (white-label).

## Pricing
€49-199/report or €99-499/month white-label subscription. Pricing psychology: sell the monthly sub as the anchor; single reports are the gateway.

## Channels
- LinkedIn (agency owners)
- Cold email to agencies
- Facebook agency groups
- Upwork (audit gigs)

## Free Tool Stack (0 budget)
- AeroSearch/Syntiva-style AI audit
- Screaming Frog (free tier)
- Canva (brand kit)
- Stripe

## 7-Day Launch Plan
- **Day 1** — Build the branded audit template in Canva.
- **Day 2** — Wire the AI audit + PDF export.
- **Day 3** — Cold-email 20 agencies: "we white-label your SEO audits."
- **Day 4** — Close 1 pilot; deliver branded PDF.
- **Day 5** — Build the €99/mo white-label sub tier.
- **Day 6** — Post a sample audit (anonymized) on LinkedIn.
- **Day 7** — Case-study the pilot; use in next 30 emails.

## AI Execution
An AI agent crawls the target site, runs the audit, writes a branded PDF with prioritized fixes, and delivers it via a client portal. It also drafts the outreach to agencies. Semi-autonomous; a human closes the white-label deal.
