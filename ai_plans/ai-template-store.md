# Business Kit: AI-Built Template Store

## Problem
People want productivity systems (CRMs, content calendars, second brains) but can't build them and resent paying agencies.

## Audience
Founders, students, and productivity nerds who buy ready-made systems.

## Offer
An AI that designs Notion / Sheets / Airtable templates, exports them, writes the sales copy, and lists them on Gumroad. The differentiator: a new template drops every few days, so the store always feels alive.

## Pricing
€9-29 each, €79 vault. Pricing psychology: the €79 vault is the anchor; individual templates are the gateway.

## Channels
- Gumroad (primary)
- ProductHunt (launch the vault)
- Reddit r/Notion
- Pinterest (template previews)

## Free Tool Stack (0 budget)
- Notion (free)
- Gumroad (free)
- Canva (previews)
- Hermes (design + copy)

## 7-Day Launch Plan
- **Day 1** — Pick 3 template angles (freelance CRM, content calendar, reading tracker).
- **Day 2** — AI builds them in Notion; export as templates.
- **Day 3** — Write copy + shoot previews in Canva.
- **Day 4** — Set up Gumroad; list 3.
- **Day 5** — Post in r/Notion + 5 Pinterest pins.
- **Day 6** — Build the €79 vault (bundle 10).
- **Day 7** — ProductHunt ship-the-vault post; engage comments.

## AI Execution
An AI agent designs each template, exports it, generates preview images and copy, and publishes to Gumroad via API. It monitors categories for gaps and ships a new template weekly. A human only cashes out.

## One-Line Pitch
Templates that do the thinking so you can do the work.
