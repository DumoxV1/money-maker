# Business Kit: AI Royalty-Free Sample Packs

## Problem
Music producers need fresh, cleared royalty-free loops, but buying packs is a recurring cost and making them is slow.

## Audience
Beatmakers, indie game devs, and content creators needing SFX/loops.

## Offer
An AI that generates genre/BPM/key-tagged loop & SFX packs, packages them with artwork, and sells them on sample marketplaces.

## Pricing
€9-49/pack, plus catalog royalties. Pricing psychology: cheap per-pack to build the library; the recurring catalog is the earner.

## Channels
- Myloopkits / Loudly
- Splice-style marketplaces
- YouTube demos
- Reddit r/WeAreTheMusicMakers

## Free Tool Stack (0 budget)
- Loudly / Magenta-style AI
- DAW export (free)
- Gumroad
- Hermes (copy/artwork)

## 7-Day Launch Plan
- **Day 1** — Pick 3 genres (e.g. lo-fi, trap, ambient). Set marketplace accounts.
- **Day 2** — AI generates 20 loops for genre 1; tag BPM/key.
- **Day 3** — Master + build pack artwork; list on Gumroad + Myloopkits.
- **Day 4** — Same for genres 2 and 3.
- **Day 5** — Upload a YouTube demo mix.
- **Day 6** — Post in r/WeAreTheMusicMakers with a free loop.
- **Day 7** — Schedule next week's packs.

## AI Execution
An AI agent prompts genre/BPM/key loop generation, tags and masters them, builds pack artwork, and lists on sample marketplaces via API. New packs ship weekly, compounding the catalog.

## One-Line Pitch
AI makes the loops, you own the catalog. Cleared for commercial use.
