# Business Kit: AI Retail/Online Arbitrage

## Problem
Genuine discounted retail goods sell for more on marketplaces, but the deal-scanning and listing grunt work filters out almost everyone.

## Audience
You, as the operator. Buyers on Amazon, bol.com, eBay.

## Offer
An AI deal engine that monitors retail clearance + online arbitrage feeds, scores margin after fees, and routes winning items to fulfillment (FBA / bol.com). You supply a small float; the AI does the scouting and listing.

## Pricing
€300-700/month starting, scales with capital. Pricing psychology: it's a margin business — show the ROI on a €400 float, not the effort.

## Channels
- Amazon FBA
- bol.com
- eBay
- Facebook Marketplace (local flips)

## Free Tool Stack (0 budget)
- Keepa (price history)
- ScanPower / scanner
- Seller app
- Hermes (listing copy)

## 7-Day Launch Plan
- **Day 1** — Set up seller accounts (Amazon/bol). Get a €400 float.
- **Day 2** — Configure the AI deal scanner on 3 categories.
- **Day 3** — AI scores 20 deals; you approve 5.
- **Day 4** — Buy + ship to FBA; AI writes listings.
- **Day 5** — List on a second marketplace.
- **Day 6** — Review sell-through; tune the margin threshold.
- **Day 7** — Reinvest first profits; scale the scan window.

## AI Execution
An AI agent monitors deal feeds, scores margin after fees, and routes to fulfillment; it writes listings and reprices. A human ships/inspects optionally. An autonomous deal engine.

## One-Line Pitch
Buy low, sell higher — the oldest quiet money, AI-scaled.
