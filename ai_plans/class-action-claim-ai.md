# Business Kit: AI Class-Action Claim Filing

## Problem
People are owed settlement money from data breaches, defective products, and investor fraud — but they miss deadlines or never learn a case exists.

## Audience
Consumers in active settlements, data-breach victims, and investors.

## Offer
An AI service that scrapes open class-action settlements, matches them to a client's known purchases/employers, autofills the claim forms, and files them — for a 10-15% cut of the payout.

## Pricing
€20-50/claim or 10-15% of payout. Pricing psychology: pure contingency — client pays nothing unless they get paid, so "yes" is easy.

## Channels
- Reddit r/legaladvice
- Facebook settlement groups
- A content site ranking for "[company] settlement 2026"
- Newsletter

## Free Tool Stack (0 budget)
- openclassactions.com (source)
- Notion (tracker)
- Calendly (intake)
- Stripe (payout split)

## 7-Day Launch Plan
- **Day 1** — Scrape 20 open settlements; build a Notion tracker with deadlines.
- **Day 2** — Write the intake form (what purchases/employers they had).
- **Day 3** — Post in r/legaladvice: "you may be owed $ from X — I'll file it."
- **Day 4** — Build a simple landing page (GitHub Pages).
- **Day 5** — Cold-post in 5 Facebook settlement groups.
- **Day 6** — File 3 pilot claims for early signups.
- **Day 7** — Launch a free weekly "new settlements" newsletter.

## AI Execution
An AI agent scrapes open settlements daily, matches them against client data, autofills claim forms via browser automation, and tracks deadlines in a database. It only escalates edge cases to a human. Fully autonomous filing.

## One-Line Pitch
Money already awarded to you — we just file the paperwork.
