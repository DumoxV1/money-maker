# Business Kit: AI KDP Low-Content Books

## Problem
Low-content books (coloring, sudoku, planners, journals) sell steadily on Amazon, but manual production is slow and template-heavy — so most people publish 2 books and quit.

## Audience
Self-pub buyers on Amazon; niche hobbyists (mandala, kids, pets, fitness, language learners).

## Offer
An AI that generates interior pages + KDP-compliant covers + Pinterest mockups, then publishes print-ready books to KDP via API. Volume is the strategy: 10+ titles/week compounding.

## Pricing
€0 to produce; royalties €2-8/book. Pricing psychology: per-book margin is small — the moat is catalog size, so price at the market median and out-publish everyone.

## Channels
- Amazon KDP (primary)
- Pinterest (mockup pins)
- Etsy POD (variants)
- TikTok #kdp

## Free Tool Stack (0 budget)
- ColorCraft/Book2Color-style AI (or Hermes + image gen)
- Canva (covers/mockups)
- KDP API
- Pinterest scheduler (free)

## 7-Day Launch Plan
- **Day 1** — Pick 3 micro-niches (e.g. "dog mom coloring", "senior sudoku"). Set KDP account.
- **Day 2** — AI generates 30 interiors + 3 covers for niche 1.
- **Day 3** — Export print-ready PDFs; upload via KDP API.
- **Day 4** — Same for niches 2 and 3.
- **Day 5** — Build 30 Pinterest mockup pins.
- **Day 6** — Schedule pins; post a TikTok of the "make a book" process.
- **Day 7** — Review KDP dashboard; queue next 10 titles.

## AI Execution
An AI agent picks a niche, generates 30+ interior pages and a cover, exports print-ready PDFs, and publishes to KDP via API. It batches 10+ titles/week and refreshes Pinterest pins. A human only connects the KDP payout.

## One-Line Pitch
AI draws the pages, KDP prints and pays. Catalog compounds while you sleep.
