# Business Kit: Public-Domain Republishing

## Problem
Thousands of books and artworks are free in the public domain but buried, poorly formatted, and undiscoverable — so their commercial potential is wasted.

## Audience
Readers, teachers, and niche hobbyists who want clean editions, annotated versions, or public-domain-inspired merch.

## Offer
An AI curation engine that fetches public-domain texts (Project Gutenberg), cleans and formats them, generates covers, and publishes them as eBooks (KDP), print-on-demand, and licensed merch (Printful). Volume is the strategy: 100+ titles compounding.

## Pricing
€3-15 per eBook; margin on POD and merch. Pricing psychology: price at the cost of a coffee; the moat is catalog size and clean formatting, not per-title margin.

## Channels
- Amazon KDP (primary)
- Etsy (POD merch, annotated editions)
- Pinterest (quote cards from texts)
- Project Gutenberg curation lists

## Free Tool Stack (0 budget)
- Gutenberg.org (source)
- Hermes (clean/format/annotate)
- Canva (covers)
- KDP / Printful (free to publish)

## 7-Day Launch Plan
- **Day 1** — Pick a theme (e.g. "Victorian ghost stories"). Pull 20 titles from Gutenberg.
- **Day 2** — AI cleans formatting + adds a 1-page intro to each.
- **Day 3** — Generate 20 covers in Canva.
- **Day 4** — Set up KDP account; upload 5 titles.
- **Day 5** — Create 5 POD quote-notebooks on Etsy/Printful.
- **Day 6** — Schedule 30 Pinterest pins from notable quotes.
- **Day 7** — Publish the remaining 15 titles; track rank.

## AI Execution
An AI agent fetches public-domain texts, normalizes them, writes intros/annotations, generates covers, and publishes to KDP and Etsy via API. It expands the catalog weekly and refreshes Pinterest pins. A human only connects the store payouts.

## One-Line Pitch
Free raw material, formatted and sold forever.
