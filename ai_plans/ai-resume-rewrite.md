# Business Kit: AI Resume & Cover Letter Rewrites

## Problem
Job seekers get filtered out by ATS systems and weak resumes, even when they're qualified.

## Audience
Career changers, recent grads, and laid-off professionals actively applying.

## Offer
An AI prompt-chain that ingests a client's resume + target role, rewrites it for ATS and impact, and delivers a polished .docx plus a tailored cover letter. Optional 30-min strategy call (AI-booked via Cal.com).

## Pricing

## AI Execution
An AI agent receives the client's resume, runs it through a rewrite prompt-chain, produces the .docx and cover letter, and books the optional call via Cal.com. A human does only optional final QA. Fulfillment is fully automated.
