# Business Kit: AI Domain Flipping & Parking

## Problem
Expired or underused domains carry real traffic and keyword value that most people price blindly or ignore entirely.

## Audience
You, as the operator. Buyers via Afternic, Sedo, GoDaddy Auctions.

## Offer
An AI that scans expiring domains for traffic/keyword/backlink signals, appraises them, bids via the registrar API, and either flips them or parks them with ad revenue.

## Pricing
€50-5000/flip; parking-ad micro-income while held. Pricing psychology: the win is buying mispriced — show the appraisal delta, not the holding cost.

## Channels
- Afternic
- Sedo
- GoDaddy auctions
- Twitter/X domain flips

## Free Tool Stack (0 budget)
- Expired-domain scanners
- Parking-ad networks
- GoDaddy/Namecheap API
- Hermes (appraisal copy)

## 7-Day Launch Plan
- **Day 1** — Pick a keyword niche (e.g. "local trades + city").
- **Day 2** — AI scans 200 expiring domains; scores top 20.
- **Day 3** — Bid on 5 via registrar API.
- **Day 4** — Park them with ad code.
- **Day 5** — List 3 on Afternic/Sedo.
- **Day 6** — Write flip blurbs (AI) for Twitter.
- **Day 7** — Reinvest first sale into 5 more bids.

## AI Execution
An AI agent scans expiring domains for traffic/keywords, appraises them, bids via the registrar API, and parks them with ads. It lists winners on marketplaces and negotiates inbound offers. Autonomous portfolio.

## One-Line Pitch
