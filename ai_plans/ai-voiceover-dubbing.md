# Business Kit: AI Voiceover & Dubbing Service

## Problem
Creators and course makers need natural voiceovers and multilingual dubs but studios are slow and pricey.

## Audience
YouTubers, course creators, and indie game devs producing regular video.

## Offer
An AI voiceover + dubbing service: ingest the script, generate natural narration (ElevenLabs), optionally dub into 3 languages, and deliver edited files. Turnaround is same-day because there's no human recording.

## Pricing
€0.10-0.20/word or €50-300/project. Pricing psychology: price per-word low to win volume; bundle "3-language dub" at a premium.

## Channels
- Fiverr
- YouTube comments (offer on relevant videos)
- Reddit r/NewTubers
- Twitter/X creator threads

## Free Tool Stack (0 budget)
- ElevenLabs (free tier)
- Descript (edit)
- CapCut (assembly)

## 7-Day Launch Plan
- **Day 1** — Create Fiverr gig + samples (AI voices reading 3 styles).
- **Day 2** — Post a "AI voice vs studio" comparison on YouTube.
- **Day 3** — Comment on 20 creator videos offering a free 30-sec sample.
- **Day 4** — Build a 3-language dub demo.
- **Day 5** — List on a second marketplace (Reddit services).
- **Day 6** — DM 20 small creators with a pilot offer.
- **Day 7** — Deliver 2 paid samples; collect testimonials.

## AI Execution
An AI agent ingests the script, generates the voice, edits in Descript, and delivers the file — fully autonomous fulfillment. It can also batch-dub into multiple languages. A human only handles disputes.

## One-Line Pitch
Studio-quality voice, same-day turnaround, fraction of the cost.
