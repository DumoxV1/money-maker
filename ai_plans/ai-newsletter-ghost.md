# Business Kit: AI Newsletter Ghostwriting Service

## Problem
Creators and influencers want a consistent newsletter but can't write weekly; hiring a ghostwriter is pricey and slow.

## Audience
Substack/Beehiiv creators, coaches, and indie operators with an existing audience.

## Offer
An AI that researches the niche, writes the weekly newsletter, and auto-publishes to Substack/Beehiiv via API — under the creator's name.

## Pricing
€99-499/month retainer. Pricing psychology: cheaper than a human ghostwriter, sold as "never miss a send."

## Channels
- Substack writer directory
- Twitter/X creators
- Cold DMs to creators
- Reddit r/Substack

## Free Tool Stack (0 budget)
- Hermes (research + write)
- Substack/Beehiiv API
- Zapier
- Canva

## 7-Day Launch Plan
- **Day 1** — List 30 creators in one niche; build the pitch.
- **Day 2** — AI writes a sample newsletter for a target creator.
- **Day 3** — Cold-DM 20 creators with the sample.
- **Day 4** — Close 1 pilot retainer.
- **Day 5** — Wire the Substack/Beehiiv auto-publish.
- **Day 6** — Queue 4 weeks of content.
- **Day 7** — Deliver the first auto-published issue; ask for testimonial.

## AI Execution
An AI agent monitors the creator's niche, drafts the newsletter, queues it, and publishes via the Substack/Beehiiv API on schedule. It A/B tests subject lines. A human only approves the voice calibration.

## One-Line Pitch
Your name on a weekly newsletter you never wrote. Retainer, autopiloted.
